Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-14

Forge is a workout tracker. This policy says what Forge stores about you, why, who else handles it, and how to get it deleted. The short version: Forge keeps what you enter so it can plan and track your training, it does not sell your data, it shows no ads, and it does not send your data to any AI service.

What Forge collects

Forge only has what you give it and what the app records while you use it.

  • Account details: your email address, the name you choose to display, and a password. The password is stored as a one-way hash by our authentication provider; nobody at Forge can read it.
  • Profile details you enter during setup or in Settings: gender, date of birth, body weight and body measurements, training goal, experience level, how often you train, the equipment you have, and your starting lifts.
  • Training data: the workouts you log, every set (weight, reps, effort, duration, distance), personal records, routines you create, weekly plans, streaks, badges, and any notes you attach to an exercise.
  • Purchase status: whether you have an active Forge Pro subscription and when it renews or ends. Forge never receives your card number or other payment details; those stay with Apple or Google.
  • Diagnostics: if the app hits an error, Forge records the error message and where in the app it happened, together with the app version and your phone's operating system version. Forge also counts a small number of events, such as the app being opened, a setup step being reached, a workout being finished, or the upgrade screen being viewed. These records contain no workout contents and nothing you typed.

What Forge does not collect

  • Your location.
  • Your contacts, photos, microphone or camera.
  • Advertising identifiers. Forge has no ads and no advertising or cross-app tracking software in it.
  • Health records from Apple Health, Health Connect or any other source.

How Forge uses it

  • To build your workouts and weekly plan, suggest what to lift next, estimate recovery, and show your progress. All of this is calculated by rules built into the app. Your data is not sent to any AI or machine-learning service.
  • To keep your training history available when you sign in on another device or reinstall the app.
  • To unlock Forge Pro features when you have a subscription.
  • To find and fix crashes, and to understand in aggregate which parts of the app are used.
  • To answer you when you contact support.

Who else handles your data

Forge uses a small number of service providers to run. They process data on Forge's behalf and only for the purposes below. Forge does not sell or rent your data to anyone, and does not share it for advertising.

  • Supabase hosts the Forge database and sign-in system. Your account, profile, training data and diagnostics are stored there.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription status. It receives a random account identifier and your purchase history for Forge, not your email, name or training data.
  • Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) process payments and deliver the app. Their own privacy policies cover that processing.
  • Expo delivers app updates. When the app checks for an update, Expo's servers see the request, as any web server would.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

Your data is stored on Supabase's cloud infrastructure, which may be in a different country from the one you live in. Connections between the app and the server are encrypted. Every table that holds personal data is protected by access rules that let an account read and change only its own rows.

Some data is also kept on your phone so the app works without a connection: a workout in progress, workouts waiting to be uploaded, and your app preferences. Uninstalling the app removes it.

No system is perfectly secure. If Forge ever learns of a breach affecting your data, you will be told as the law requires.

How long it is kept

Forge keeps your data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, training data, purchase status and diagnostics are deleted from the live database immediately, and from any backups within 30 days. Records that Apple, Google or RevenueCat hold about a purchase are kept by them under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

  • See and correct your data: everything you entered is visible and editable in the app, under Settings.
  • Get a copy: ask using the contact details below and you will be sent a copy of your data in a spreadsheet-readable format. Forge Pro also includes an export button in Settings → Account.
  • Clear your training history but keep your account: Settings → Account → Start Fresh.
  • Delete everything: Settings → Account → Delete Account. This is permanent. If you can't open the app, you can ask for deletion using the contact details below.
  • Depending on where you live, for example in the EU, the UK or California, you may have further legal rights, including the right to object to or restrict processing and to complain to your data-protection authority. Forge will honour a valid request wherever you live.

Children

Forge is not intended for anyone under 13, or under 16 in countries where that is the age of digital consent. If you believe a child has created an account, get in touch and it will be removed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects how your data is used, the change will be announced in the app before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
