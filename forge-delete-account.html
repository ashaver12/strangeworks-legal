Delete Your Forge Account

Last updated 2026-11-14

You can delete your Forge account and all the data attached to it at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

In the app

  • Open Forge and go to Settings.
  • Tap Account.
  • Tap Delete Account and confirm.

If you can't open the app

Email strangeworksstudio@gmail.com from the address your account uses and ask for it to be deleted. You will get a confirmation when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in details.
  • Your profile: name, body weight and measurements, goal, equipment and starting lifts.
  • All training data: workouts, sets, personal records, routines, weekly plans, streaks, badges and notes.
  • Diagnostic records linked to your account.

What is not deleted

  • An active subscription. Deleting your account does not cancel it. Cancel first in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.
  • Purchase records held by Apple, Google and our subscription provider, which they keep under their own policies.

How long it takes

Deleting from inside the app takes effect immediately. A request made by email is completed within 30 days. Copies in backups are removed within 30 days.

Keep your account, clear your history

If you only want to wipe your workout history and start again, use Settings → Account → Start Fresh instead. Your account, profile and subscription are kept.
