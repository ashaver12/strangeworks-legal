Forge Support

Forge is a workout tracker that plans your training and tells you what to lift next.

Get help

Email strangeworksstudio@gmail.com. Include your phone model and the version shown at the bottom of Settings.

Subscriptions

Forge Pro is billed by Apple or Google. Manage or cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings. If Pro is missing after a reinstall or on a new phone, open Settings → Account → Restore Purchases.
