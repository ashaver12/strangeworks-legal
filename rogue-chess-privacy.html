Rogue Chess: The Devil's Gambit Privacy Policy

Strange Works Studio · Effective 13 November 2026

Rogue Chess keeps your game progress on your device. It has no accounts, no cloud save, no analytics, and no advertising. Its one purchase is handled by Google Play.

What Rogue Chess stores

Rogue Chess saves your game progress and preferences on your device: your current run and board position, held cheats, earned unlocks, ascension progress, recent run history, daily puzzle progress and the dates of the seals you have earned, sound, music, vibration and piece-style settings, and hints you have dismissed. It also keeps one backup copy of the save on your device so a damaged save can be recovered.

The game uses your device's date to choose the daily puzzle. Daily seals have no cash value.

Rogue Chess does not ask you to create an account, does not sign you in to any service, and does not operate a cloud-save service. We do not receive your progress and cannot see it.

The full-game purchase

The first act is free. The rest of the game is a single one-time purchase made through Google Play.

  • Google processes the payment and keeps the purchase record under Google's privacy policy. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • The game asks Google Play whether the full game has been bought on the Google account signed in to your device. It stores only the answer, yes or no, on your device.
  • That check, and the purchase itself, need an internet connection. The rest of the game plays offline.

To make this possible the app includes Google's Play Billing Library. That library communicates with Google Play, and Google may collect information through it, such as purchase history and diagnostic data, under its own policy. The game's own code sends nothing to us or to anyone else, and we operate no server.

What Rogue Chess does not collect

Rogue Chess does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. It runs no analytics or crash-reporting service of its own, shows no advertising, has no social or login integrations, and performs no tracking or profiling of any kind.

It does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Permissions

  • Vibration, for optional feedback on captures and checks. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Google Play billing, Internet and network state, all required by Google's Play Billing Library for the purchase described above.

Android backup

Android may include app data in its own backup and restore services, depending on your device and Google account settings. That service belongs to your device and Google, not to us, and is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can control it in your Android settings.

If you contact support

If you email us, your email provider and ours transmit your address, your message and anything you attach. We use that only to answer you, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, or any information the question does not need.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress stays on your device until it is replaced or removed.

  • Clearing the app's storage in Android's app settings erases all local progress and settings.
  • Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data, though Android's backup service may restore it if you reinstall.
  • Neither of those affects your purchase. Google Play keeps the purchase record, and the full game is restored from it when you reinstall while signed in to the same Google account.

Because we never receive your progress, there is nothing on our side to delete. To ask about correspondence you sent to support, email us at the address below. For your purchase record, refunds and payment data, use your Google Play account.

Children

Rogue Chess does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support email, contact us and we will delete that correspondence.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Rogue Chess changes what data the game handles, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Any change is published on this page with a new effective date. The date at the top always reflects the version currently in force.
