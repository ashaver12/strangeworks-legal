Breaker Privacy Policy

Strange Works Studio · Effective 13 November 2026

Breaker keeps your game progress on your device. It has no accounts, no cloud save, no analytics, and no advertising.

What Breaker stores

Breaker saves your game progress and preferences on your device: unlocked ships, upgrades, cores, research, records, settings and a resumable run. This stays on your device.

Breaker does not ask you to create an account, does not sign you in to any service, and does not operate a cloud-save service. We do not receive your progress and cannot see it.

What Breaker does not collect

Breaker does not collect, transmit or sell personal information. It runs no analytics or crash-reporting service, shows no advertising, has no social or login integrations, and performs no tracking or profiling of any kind.

The released app includes Google's Mobile Ads library, because a future version may offer optional rewarded ads. In this version that library is never started, never requests an ad, and never requests consent, so it does not collect or transmit anything. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

The app requests only the Internet permission, which Android requires for the technology Breaker is built on. Breaker does not use it to send your data anywhere.

Android backup

Android may include app data in its own backup and restore services, depending on your device and Google account settings. That service belongs to your device and Google, not to us, and is governed by Google's privacy policy. You can control it in your Android settings.

Save files you export

Breaker lets you export a save file through your device's document picker. The file is written only where you choose to put it.

  • If you save it to a cloud-storage provider, that provider handles the file under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • An exported file contains your game progress and is not encrypted or password protected. Anyone with access to the file can restore it.
  • Restoring a file replaces your current progress. Breaker keeps one previous-save recovery copy inside the app so a mistaken restore can be undone.

If you contact support

If you email us, your email provider and ours transmit your address, your message and anything you attach. We use that only to answer you, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

Please do not send passwords, verification codes, or any information the question does not need.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress stays on your device until it is replaced or removed.

  • System → Factory reset inside the game erases the app's local progress and its recovery copy.
  • Uninstalling the app generally removes its local data, though Android's backup service may restore it if you reinstall.
  • Factory reset does not delete save files you exported, or copies held by a cloud provider. Delete those wherever you saved them.

Because we never receive your progress, there is nothing on our side to delete. To ask about correspondence you sent to support, email us at the address below.

Children

Breaker does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support email, contact us and we will delete that correspondence.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Breaker adds optional rewarded advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and will describe what the advertising provider processes and the choices available to you.

Any change is published on this page with a new effective date. The date at the top always reflects the version currently in force.
